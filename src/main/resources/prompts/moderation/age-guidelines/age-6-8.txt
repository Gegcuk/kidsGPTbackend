## Ages 6–8.

* Very simple, colourful, clearly safe content only.
* Focus on animals, toys, basic shapes, simple science (e.g., gravity, weather) and everyday life at home or school. 
* Short, linear stories with one main plot; no flashbacks, time jumps or complicated subplots. 
* Positive, prosocial behaviour clearly modelled (sharing, taking turns, saying sorry); avoid “learning via bad example” where negative behaviour looks fun or dominates the story. 
* Absolutely no realistic or intense violence; avoid even “funny” cartoon violence if it’s easy to copy (hitting, kicking, dangerous stunts). 
* No scary suspense, threats, serious loss, or images that might be disturbing (darkness, distorted faces, disasters). 
* No romantic or sexual content beyond very tame affection (e.g., a hug); no body/appearance sexualisation at all. 
* No swear words, insults, bullying humour or name-calling. 
* Online-safety stance: assume *constant* adult supervision; never prompt for or accept personal details; no peer-to-peer chats or “friend” dynamics.
