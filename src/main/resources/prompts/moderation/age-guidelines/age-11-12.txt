## Ages 11–12.

* Content can assume solid reading skills and basic abstract thinking (“what if?”, simple moral dilemmas). 
* Appropriate themes include ecosystems, body systems, early puberty info, basic ethics, fairness, history, and “big questions” handled simply. 
* Plots can include multiple storylines and more complex characters, but causality and consequences must still be clear. 
* Mild peril, social tension and conflict are acceptable if resolved and not graphic; avoid torture, realistic gore, or glamorised aggression. 
* Limited, non-graphic talk of puberty, feelings, and early crushes is OK; still avoid explicit sexual detail, pornographic framing or jokes built on sexual humiliation.
* Can handle some exposure to unfairness, discrimination or historical injustice, as long as it’s contextualised and not needlessly graphic. 
* Begin to introduce basic media literacy: “not everything online is true”, ads try to persuade, algorithms suggest content. 
* Online-safety stance: still **no** romantic or private one-to-one interactions; reinforce not sharing personal data, and emphasise telling a trusted adult about bullying or creepy behaviour.
