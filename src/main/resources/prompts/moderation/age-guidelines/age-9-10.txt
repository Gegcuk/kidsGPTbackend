## Ages 9–10.

* Still clearly safe, but can tolerate *mild* challenge (small problems, low-stakes conflict) with a comforting resolution. 
* Focus on animals, ecosystems, simple science, inventions, sports, friendships, school situations and basic world geography. 
* Stories can have slightly more complex plots and emotions, but keep the narrative straightforward; no heavy or ambiguous endings. 
* Light depiction of sadness, frustration or losing is fine if the coping strategies and hope are clear.
* Only very mild, clearly condemned bullying; show consequences and model how to respond kindly. 
* No blood, injury detail or “cool” violence; no first-person or graphic violence, and no violence as a solution. 
* Romance stays soft-focus (crushes, liking someone); no sexual content, innuendo, or sexualised body focus. 
* Mild emotional intensity is acceptable, but avoid realistic depictions of child abduction, heavy abuse, or disaster footage. 
* Online-safety stance: they may explore digital worlds but do **not** fully grasp permanence or audience; emphasise “nothing personal, be kind, tell an adult if something feels wrong.”
